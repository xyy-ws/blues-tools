# AI Study Notes Templates

## 1) Flash Mode (30s)

```md
# {{date}} - Flash Log
- Learned: {{one_line_takeaway}}
- Unclear: {{one_confusion}}
- Next: {{one_action}}

Next Review: {{date_or_trigger}}
```

## 2) Standard Mode (2-3 min)

```md
# {{date}} - {{topic}}
Tags: #ai-learning

## Key Points
- {{point_1}}
- {{point_2}}
- {{point_3}}

## Misconceptions / Risks
- {{risk_or_common_mistake}}

## Actionable Step
- {{concrete_step_for_tomorrow}}

## Related
- [[Concepts/{{concept_1}}]]
- [[FAQ/{{faq_topic}}]]

Next Review: {{date_or_trigger}}
```

## 3) Deep Mode (paper/research)

```md
# {{date}} - Deep Dive: {{title}}
Source: {{url_or_citation}}
Tags: #ai-learning #research

## What it solves
- {{problem_statement}}

## Method (plain language)
- {{method_summary}}

## Why it works
- {{mechanism}}

## Limits / Failure Cases
- {{limit_1}}
- {{limit_2}}

## Transfer to my workflow (Agent-first)
- {{how_to_apply}}
- {{first_small_experiment}}

## Q&A Capture
- Q: {{user_question}}
- A: {{assistant_answer_short}}

Next Review: {{date_or_trigger}}
```
